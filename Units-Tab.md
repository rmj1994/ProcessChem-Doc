---
title: Units
---

Choose the unit for each quantity separately. There is no global SI/Imperial switch, because engineers mix bar with °C and psi with °F.

Changing a unit converts what you have already typed rather than reinterpreting it, and clears any results, which were calculated in the old units.

The Normal-flow reference lives here too. It defaults to 20 °C and 1 atm and is worth checking against your instrument: "normal" conditions are not standardised, and a 0 °C reference reads about 7 % higher in mass terms.
