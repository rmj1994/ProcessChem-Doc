title: Property
---

Look up one chemical, or a mixture, at a temperature and pressure you choose.

Returns density and phase, heat capacity, viscosity, thermal and electrical conductivity, enthalpy of vaporisation, compressibility factor, surface tension, and the enthalpy and Gibbs energy of formation. Hazard and safety data — flash point, flammability limits, exposure limits, carcinogenicity — is behind its own button.

Every value names where it came from and says when it does not apply, rather than showing a number that is really an extrapolation or a phase it was never measured in. Tap the ⓘ beside any value to see its source.
