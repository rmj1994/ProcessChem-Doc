ProcessChem is a chemical process engineering calculator. This wiki is the documentation behind every ⓘ icon in the app, published so it can be read, linked to, and searched outside it too.

## Tabs

- [Property](Property-Tab) — look up one chemical or mixture's properties at a temperature and pressure
- [Thermo](Thermo-Tab) — four ways of asking what happens to a substance
- [Case](Case-Tab) — real-world scenarios, each with its own inputs
- [Units](Units-Tab) — per-quantity unit choice and the Normal-flow reference

## Thermo modes

- [Phase Equilibrium](Phase-Equilibrium) — one chemical changing state
- [Chemical Equilibrium](Chemical-Equilibrium) — a feed left to find its own minimum
- [Reaction](Reaction) — a reaction you already know
- [Electrochemical Reaction](Electrochemical-Reaction) — a reaction driven by electricity, or driving it

## Cases

- [Heater](Heater) — heats or cools a batch or stream at constant pressure
- [Vessel Heater](Vessel-Heater) — a sealed rigid vessel heated or cooled
- [Fluidized Bed](Fluidized-Bed) — sizes a fluidized bed
- [Heat Exchanger](Heat-Exchanger) — two streams exchanging heat

## About this documentation

This wiki is generated from the `docs/` folder in the app's own source repository — editing a page here directly will be overwritten the next time it's published. See the source repository for how to contribute a change.
