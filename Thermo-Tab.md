title: Thermo
---

Three ways of asking what happens to a substance, chosen from the dropdown.

**[Phase Equilibrium](Phase-Equilibrium) / Flash** — one chemical changing state: heated, cooled, compressed, expanded or throttled. Give the amount as a quantity for a sealed vessel or as a rate for a flowing stream, and the basis decides which. Finds the final state with the heat and work.

**[Chemical Equilibrium](Chemical-Equilibrium) / Minimize Gibbs** — a feed left to find its own minimum. You name the components; the app lists every compound their elements could form, you tick which may take part, and it finds the composition with the lowest total Gibbs energy. This is the mode for calcination, combustion products or ore reduction, where you do not already know what forms.

**[Reaction](Reaction)** — a reaction you already know. Name the reactants and products and the app balances the equation itself, applies your excess and conversion, and reports the full material balance with ΔH, ΔG and K.

The difference between the last two is who decides the products: Chemical Equilibrium works them out, Reaction takes them from you.
